Focus Areas: EEG, NCS, EP, Sleep Studies, and Intraoperative Neuromonitoring (IONM)
​About This Repository
​High-quality, standardized clinical protocols are rarely open-sourced in the medical technology ecosystem. Clinics and neurotechnologists often have to build their Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) from scratch.
​This repository provides a rigorously structured, open-source framework for intraoperative neuromonitoring and neurophysiology labs. These protocols are designed to align with standard clinical guidelines (including ABRET/CNIM foundations) to ensure high signal quality, patient safety, and standardized neurodiagnostic workflows.
​1. Clinical EEG Patient Preparation Protocol
​Standardized workflow for routine and continuous Electroencephalography.
​1.1 Pre-Procedural Patient Assessment
​Verify patient identity and cross-reference with the clinical order.
​Review patient history for skull defects, recent neurosurgery, infectious diseases, or communicable skin conditions.
​Explain the procedure to the patient to ensure cooperation and reduce muscle artifact (EMG) interference.
​1.2 Measurement and Marking (10-20 System)
​Measure the nasion to inion distance to establish the midline sagittal plane.
​Measure pre-auricular to pre-auricular distance across the vertex (Cz).
​Calculate and mark 10% and 20% intervals using a skin-safe marking pencil to ensure precise, reproducible electrode placement.
​1.3 Skin Preparation (Impedance Reduction)
​Part the hair at each marked site.
​Lightly abrade the scalp using a skin prep gel (e.g., NuPrep) on a cotton-tipped applicator to remove dead skin cells and sebum.
​Target: Keep skin impedances strictly below 5 kOhms (and ideally balanced across all channels) to maximize common-mode rejection and reduce environmental noise.
​1.4 Electrode Application
​Apply conductive paste (e.g., Ten20) to the gold-cup or Ag/AgCl electrodes.
​Place the electrode firmly on the prepared site.
​Secure with a small gauze square or collodion depending on the duration of the monitoring (routine vs. long-term).
​Bundle lead wires neatly to prevent triboelectric artifacts from cable movement.
